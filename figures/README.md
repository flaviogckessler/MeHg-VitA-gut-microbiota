# Main figures

## Output policy

- Generate scripted figures as lightweight, readable PNG files.
- Do not generate new TIFF files; preserve manually supplied TIFF files as reference artifacts.

- Panel B (F1, PND21; females and males) uses the same batch 1 [`taxon-relative-abundance table`](../results/taxon_relative_abundance/batch01_iontorrent_dams-f0_f1-pnd21_taxon_relative_abundance.csv) and [`sample manifest`](../data/metadata/batch01_iontorrent_dams-f0_f1-pnd21/sample_manifest.csv). It is generated by [`Figure_4/generate_figure_4_b.R`](Figure_4/generate_figure_4_b.R).
- Panel C (F1, PND365; females and males) uses [`batch02_illumina-miseq_f1-f2_pnd365_taxon_relative_abundance.csv`](../results/taxon_relative_abundance/batch02_illumina-miseq_f1-f2_pnd365_taxon_relative_abundance.csv) and the corresponding canonical [`sample_manifest.csv`](../data/metadata/batch02_illumina-miseq_f1-f2_pnd365/sample_manifest.csv). ### Figure 5

- Panels A-C use [`Figure_5/Figure_5_A-C_SourceData.pzfx`](Figure_5/Figure_5_A-C_SourceData.pzfx). They were generated in GraphPad Prism; panels A-B are exported as [`Figure_5_AB.png`](Figure_5/output/Figure_5_AB.png) and panel C as [`Figure_5_C.png`](Figure_5/output/Figure_5_C.png).
- Panel D uses the F1 THg measurements in [`metadata_thg_hplc.csv`](../data/metadata/batch01_iontorrent_dams-f0_f1-pnd21/metadata_thg_hplc.csv), primary-analysis inclusion and sex labels from the corresponding canonical [`sample_manifest.csv`](../data/metadata/batch01_iontorrent_dams-f0_f1-pnd21/sample_manifest.csv), and the `g__Dorea` row from [`batch01_iontorrent_dams-f0_f1-pnd21_taxon_relative_abundance.csv`](../results/taxon_relative_abundance/batch01_iontorrent_dams-f0_f1-pnd21_taxon_relative_abundance.csv). It is generated by [`generate_figure_5_d.R`](Figure_5/generate_figure_5_d.R), a portable base-R refactor of [`Correlacoes_Metadados.R`](../scripts/legacy_scripts/Correlacoes_Metadados.R).
- The panel D generator excludes `10F` and `22F` according to `include_primary_analysis`, recomputes the sex-stratified Spearman correlations, checks them against the legacy statistical checkpoints, and writes [`Figure5D_generated.png`](Figure_5/output/Figure5D_generated.png). The supplied `Dorea_SEX_hg_correlation_nonparametric.tiff` was used as the standalone visual reference during migration; [`Figure_5_FinalFigure.png`](Figure_5/output/Figure_5_FinalFigure.png) is the retained manually assembled full-figure reference composite.

### Figure 6

- The planned functional-prediction figure will use the EC, KO, and MetaCyc abundance matrices under [`results/functional_prediction/`](../results/functional_prediction/) for both sequencing batches. The per-sample `weighted_nsti.tsv` files are retained as PICRUSt2 quality-control outputs rather than figure inputs.
- Batch 01 uses its exact [`picrust2_input_asv_counts.tsv`](../data/processed/batch01_iontorrent_dams-f0_f1-pnd21/picrust2_input_asv_counts.tsv) and [`picrust2_input_representative_sequences.fasta`](../data/processed/batch01_iontorrent_dams-f0_f1-pnd21/picrust2_input_representative_sequences.fasta); batch 02 uses the corresponding filtered [`count table`](../data/processed/batch02_illumina-miseq_f1-f2_pnd365/picrust2_input_asv_counts.tsv) and [`representative sequences`](../data/processed/batch02_illumina-miseq_f1-f2_pnd365/picrust2_input_representative_sequences.fasta). Their relationship to the broader canonical files is documented in [`data/processed/README.md`](../data/processed/README.md). The Linux-terminal reconstruction is [`picrust2_workflow.md`](../scripts/04_functional_prediction/picrust2_workflow.md), derived from the unchanged [`Script_picrust_mehg.txt`](../scripts/legacy_scripts/Script_picrust_mehg.txt) command log.
- The historical downstream analysis reference is [`Picrust_mestrado_Alberto.R`](../scripts/Picrust_mestrado_Alberto.R). It is not yet a portable Figure 6 generator and retains absolute paths and interactive assumptions; it must be refactored and validated before use.
- A future Figure 6 generator must join samples to [`sample_manifest_all_batches.csv`](../data/metadata/sample_manifest_all_batches.csv), honor `include_primary_analysis`, exclude `10F` and `22F`, and exclude the undocumented batch 02 column `t3`. No generated Figure 6 panels are claimed at this stage.
